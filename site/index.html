Akuma Esports presents

Akuma Super Series
Draft Invitational Championship

A free community League of Legends tournament for North America. Captains build their rosters in a blind-auction player draft, then 12 teams compete for the title.

Format

  • Teams: 12, drafted by captains in a blind auction
  • Swiss stage: 4 rounds, 2 wins to qualify, 8 teams advance
  • Playoffs: 8-team single elimination, best of 5
  • Draft: full fearless in every series
  • Entry: free
