Akuma Super Series

Official Rulebook

Last updated November 23, 2026. Admins may update these rules during the event; changes are announced on the Akuma Esports channels before they take effect.

1. Overview

The Akuma Super Series Draft Invitational Championship (ASSDIC) is a free community League of Legends tournament run by Akuma Esports on the North America server. Twelve captains build five-player rosters through a blind-auction player draft, then compete in a Swiss stage followed by a single-elimination playoff bracket.

  • Entry is free for all players and captains.
  • Every series is played in full fearless draft (see section 7).
  • Any prizes are announced on the Akuma Esports channels before playoffs begin.
  • All players must follow the Riot Games Terms of Service throughout the event.

2. Auction draft

  • Each of the 12 captains starts with a $50 budget, used only for the draft.
  • Players are put up for auction one lot at a time. Bids are blind: captains submit bids privately and the highest bid wins the player.
  • A captain cannot bid more than their remaining budget.
  • Each team finishes the draft with five players, the captain included.
  • Players who are not drafted form the substitute pool (see section 6).
  • Admins resolve any tied bids or draft disputes, and their decision is final.

4. Swiss stage

Two series wins qualify a team for playoffs. Eight teams advance and four are eliminated.

  1. Round 1: all 12 teams play a best-of-3 series.
  2. Round 2: 1-0 teams play each other and 0-1 teams play each other. Teams that reach 2-0 qualify.
  3. Round 3: 1-1 teams play a best-of-3; winners qualify at 2-1. The 0-2 teams play a best-of-1 round robin: first place survives to Round 4, and second and third place are eliminated.
  4. Round 4 (last chance): the 1-2 teams and the round-robin winner play a best-of-3. Winners qualify; losers are eliminated.

Pairings avoid rematches where possible. If three teams finish the round robin 1-1, the winner is decided by total game time won (fastest combined win time ranks first). If that is still tied, admins decide by coin flip.

5. Playoffs

  • Eight teams play a single-elimination bracket. Every series is best of 5.
  • Seeds 1 to 3 go to 2-0 teams, seeds 4 to 6 to 2-1 teams, and seeds 7 and 8 to Round 4 winners.
  • Within each group, teams are ordered by game differential across the Swiss stage, then by head-to-head result, then by coin flip.
  • The higher seed chooses side in game 1.

6. Rosters and substitutes

  • Every player must use the Riot ID they registered with. Account sharing or playing on another person's account means a forfeit for that series.
  • A team may use a substitute from the undrafted pool if a rostered player cannot attend. The captain must ask an admin before the series starts, and the admin approves the substitute.
  • Substitutes can only be swapped in between games, not during one.
  • Captains may not trade players or add players from outside the event without admin approval.

7. Match setup

  • Matches are played on Summoner's Rift in lobbies created with tournament codes provided by admins.
  • Full fearless draft: every series uses Tournament Draft with full fearless rules. Once a champion has been played in a series, by either team, neither team can pick it again for the rest of that series. Bans reset each game.
  • Game 5 of a best of 5 is blind pick. There are no bans, and fearless restrictions still apply, so champions played in games 1 to 4 cannot be picked.
  • Picking a champion that has already been played in the series means a forfeit of that game, unless the mistake is caught before the game starts and the lobby is remade.
  • Both teams should be in the lobby 10 minutes before the scheduled start.
  • Side selection: the higher seed picks side in game 1 of playoff series; in the Swiss stage, admins assign game 1 sides. In later games of a series, the loser of the previous game picks side.
  • Teams have 10 minutes between games of a series.

8. Lateness and forfeits

  • A team that is not ready to play 30 minutes after the scheduled start forfeits the series.
  • A team may play with four players only if the opposing captain agrees; otherwise the lateness rule above applies.
  • Forfeited games count as losses with no game time recorded.

9. Pauses, disconnects and remakes

  • Each team may pause for a total of 10 minutes per game for disconnects or technical problems. Announce the reason in all chat.
  • If a player disconnects before first blood or before the 3-minute mark, either team may request a remake. Champion picks and bans stay the same.
  • If the game crashes or the server goes down, admins decide whether to replay the game, continue it, or award it based on the state of the game at the time.
  • Disconnects caused by a stream or broadcast problem do not affect the result of a game.

10. Conduct

  • Treat opponents, admins and viewers with respect. Harassment, hate speech, cheating, scripting, intentional feeding or win trading are not allowed.
  • Admins may issue warnings, game forfeits, series forfeits or disqualification depending on how serious the offence is.
  • Ghosting, which means watching the live broadcast of your own match to gain information, is not allowed.

11. Disputes and admin decisions

  • Captains raise any dispute with an admin as soon as possible, and before the next game starts where they can.
  • Admins decide anything these rules do not cover, and their decisions are final.
  • Results are recorded on the official tournament sheet once both captains confirm them.
